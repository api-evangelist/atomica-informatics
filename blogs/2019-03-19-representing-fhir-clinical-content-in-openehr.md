Over the past few days I’ve attempted the task of representing the FHIR Skin and Wound Assessment profiles using openEHR archetypes. I note that there are three Skin and Wound Assessment FHIR Implementation Guides available online: "Full CIMI" version – which is the one I chose to model; Federal Health Information Model (FHIM) version ; and Mitre’s ‘mini-CIMI’ version . I’m a clinical modeler, a clinician by background, so I’m always looking at how we can best represent clinical data in ways that are friendly to grassroots, non-technical clinicians of any sort.
